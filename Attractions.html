Attractions

Popular Attractions

  • Sky Dragon

    Enjoy an exciting ride on Sky Dragon! Feel the wind as you race through the sky on this thrilling roller coaster. It is perfect for adventure lovers!

  • Dream Wheel

    Take a relaxing ride on Dream Wheel and enjoy beautiful views of the park. It is a great place to take pictures and make special memories with your family.

Other attractions

  • Magic Tea Cups

    Spin around in our colorful tea cups! Magic Tea Cups is a fun ride for families and friends. Get ready for lots of laughter and fun!

  • Pirate Adventure

    Get ready for a pirate adventure! Ride our exciting pirate ship and enjoy the feeling of sailing on the ocean. Are you ready for the adventure?

  • Splash River

    Cool off with a fun ride on Splash River! Enjoy the water and exciting drops as you travel along the river. It is a perfect ride for a hot summer day.

  • Crazy Cars

    Have fun driving our bumper cars! Move around, bump into other cars, and enjoy a fun time with your friends. Who will be the best driver?
